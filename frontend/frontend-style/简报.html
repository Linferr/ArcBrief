Bangladesh holds pivotal election after 2024 uprising

Bangladesh conducts its first national election since the 2024 Gen Z-led uprising ousted PM Sheikh Hasina, with polls marked by high turnout and a direct contest between BNP’s Tarique Rahman and a Jamaat-e-Islami coalition.

Key Takeaways

  • ▪ This is Bangladesh’s first election following the removal of long-serving Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina in a youth-driven uprising in 2024.
  • ▪ The election features a major showdown between the BNP and an 11-party coalition led by Jamaat-e-Islami, under heavy security.
